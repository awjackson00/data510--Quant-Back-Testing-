Portfolio behavior varies substantially across market regimes

No portfolio in this study ranks first across all nine regimes. Mega-Cap Technology led during every expansion and recovery period tested but produced the steepest loss of any portfolio during the 2022 rate cycle. Energy did almost the opposite. ARIMA: a null result

ARIMA was tested on portfolio-level daily return series using the Box–Jenkins framework and statsmodels, not as a trading strategy but to check whether daily returns carried stable autoregressive structure. They mostly didn't — forecasts converged toward zero and did not reveal predictive structure strong enough to inform the resilience analysis. We report this as a useful null result rather than a modeling failure: the project's evidence comes from historical backtesting, risk-adjusted metrics, and regime-specific comparisons, not from forecasting.
